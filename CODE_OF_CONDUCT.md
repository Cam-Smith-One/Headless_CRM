# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Headless CRM to be a project where people can contribute, ask questions, and disagree constructively without being treated badly.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and generous with context.
- Assume good intent, but be clear about impact.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Help newer contributors get unblocked.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior in issues, PRs, or discussions.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block participation when conduct makes the project less safe or less usable for others.

For serious concerns, contact the maintainers privately through the repository security/support channels.
